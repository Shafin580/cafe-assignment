Peppy Puffin Café — HS1021 Group Assignment
============================================

Unit:     HS1021 Web Design
Trimester: T1-MT 2026
Scenario:  1 — Local Café or Restaurant
Business:  Peppy Puffin Café

Business Description:
  Peppy Puffin Café is a fictional specialty coffee café located in Birchwood,
  Melbourne, targeting students, remote workers, and coffee enthusiasts with
  single-origin brews, homemade pastries, and a cosy, welcoming atmosphere.
