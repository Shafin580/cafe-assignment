A little café with a lot of character

Peppy Puffin Café opened in 2019 on the corner of Birch Lane and Wren Street. The name came easy — puffins are cheerful, a bit colourful, and completley unfazed by rough weather. That felt right for a neighbourhood café.

We're proudly independent. No loyalty app, no drive-through, no rush. Just good coffee made by people who genuinely love it, served in a space we've put a lot of care into.

We work with local suppliers wherever we can — the sourdough comes from Clearwater Bakery down the road, and our seasonal produce comes from the Birchwood growers market every friday morning.
